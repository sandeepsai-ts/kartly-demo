Cookies and storage

Browser state: what persists, for how long, and what a fresh session loses.

Test ideas and selectors
  • Set cookies with a name, value and lifetime in seconds; #cookie-list shows what the page can read.
  • Local storage survives restarts, session storage dies with the tab; both are listed live.
  • IndexedDB write and read.
  • Session timer: start a 30 second session; the protected area disappears when the cookie expires. Useful for testing session reuse and expiry.
  • Storage events from another tab appear in #storage-log.
