Files: upload and download

File inputs, drag-drop zones, previews and generated downloads.

Test ideas and selectors
  • #file-single, #file-multi (multiple) and #file-image (accept=image/*): attach with the framework's upload API, never the native dialog.
  • Drop zone #dropzone also accepts files dragged from the desktop.
  • Simulated upload progress bar finishes in about 2 seconds.
  • Downloads are generated in the browser: #dl-txt, #dl-csv, #dl-json (Blob URLs with a download name).
