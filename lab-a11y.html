Accessibility and display

Landmarks, ARIA patterns and display preferences that change how the page renders.

Test ideas and selectors
  • Landmarks (banner, main, nav, contentinfo) and a skip link that appears on first Tab.
  • ARIA live region #live announces updates without moving focus.
  • Accordion buttons use aria-expanded; the modal traps focus and returns it on close.
  • Labels: a properly associated label versus a placeholder-only input.
  • Preferences persist in localStorage and apply on every page: dark mode, high contrast, large text, reduced motion, right-to-left layout.
