Frames and Shadow DOM

Content that lives in another document or behind a shadow root cannot be reached with ordinary selectors.

Test ideas and selectors
  • Switch into #frame-basic (also named basic-frame) before touching its input and button.
  • #frame-outer contains #frame-inner: switch twice, then switch back to the parent or default content.
  • Open shadow root in <fancy-card>: pierce with the shadow selector. The closed root in <secret-card> cannot be reached from outside.
  • Slotted content stays in the light DOM and is selectable normally.
  • Text outside the frame (#outside-result) is updated by a message from inside.
