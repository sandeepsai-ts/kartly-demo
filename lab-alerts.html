Alerts, dialogs and overlays

Browser-native dialogs, an HTML dialog element, auto-dismissing toasts and overlays that intercept clicks.

Test ideas and selectors
  • Native alert, confirm and prompt: results are written to #alert-result, #confirm-result, #prompt-result.
  • Delayed alert after 2 seconds: the test must wait for the dialog.
  • <dialog> modal with focus trap, closes with Escape or the Close button.
  • Toast disappears after 3 seconds: assert it quickly, then assert it is gone.
  • Consent overlay blocks every click until accepted (element click intercepted).
  • Delete confirmation pattern: destructive action needs a second click.
